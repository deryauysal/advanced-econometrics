Econometrics: Advanced Methods

Short lecture notes for Research Metrics 2 (MQE, LMU Munich), Winter 2026/27

Author

Derya Uysal

Preface

These notes accompany the course Econometrics: Advanced Methods (Research Metrics 2) in the Munich Graduate School of Economics MQE programme. They are deliberately short. Each chapter states the key definitions and results, works through one or two examples in detail, and ends with a computer tutorial in R. Proofs are sketched where the argument is instructive and otherwise referenced.

The notes complement the lecture slides; they do not replace the textbooks listed below.

What the course covers

The objective is to introduce fundamental estimation methods in econometrics. We spend a fair amount of time on the theoretical properties of the estimators and also discuss practical aspects of implementation. The course is not a course on causality or identification, and it is not an R course, although both appear throughout.

Software

All tutorials use R. Install a recent version of R and an editor (RStudio or Positron), then install the packages used in the notes:

install.packages(c("sandwich", "lmtest", "MASS", "nleqslv",
                   "randtoolbox", "statmod", "glmnet", "haven"))

Every tutorial can be run from top to bottom. Data sets are in the data/ folder of the course material on Moodle.

How to use the tutorials

Each tutorial first states the problems. Try them yourself before opening the solution boxes. Solutions are released after the tutorial session.
